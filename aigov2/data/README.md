# Data modules

Generated by `tools/generate_data.py` (seed 360, fixed today 2026-09-04). Every file assigns onto `window.AGC.data`. Load order does not matter except that `conversations-*.js` only merge into `AGC.data.conversations`.

Customer: Brookhaven Financial, a regional bank holding company with a broker-dealer and wealth arm (Brookhaven Securities). Three ChatGPT Enterprise workspaces: Client-Facing (Retail Banking, Wealth Management, Lending, Contact Center), Corporate (Marketing, Finance and Treasury, Human Resources, Information Technology, Legal and Compliance), Risk and Analytics (Financial Crimes, Credit Risk, Data Science). Regulatory bases are limited to PCI DSS, FINRA, SEC recordkeeping, model risk guidance and Internal policy.
